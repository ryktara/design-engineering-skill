## A. Headline

The concern model and guidance bundle improved every measured axis over the Phase 2 baseline, and nine real-project torture tests found the skill useful in each case. The blind held-out v2 set, however, met only 3 of its 8 pre-registered thresholds: independent reviewers expect concepts the bundle does not surface (mean phrase recall 0.206) and expect audit/polish modes more often than the classifier detects them (mode correct 61 %). Generalisation is better than before and still not good enough to call the retrieval stable.

Other observables: platform precise 190 / 212; inputs correct 207 / 212; negatives 203 / 212; forbidden-concern violations 7; mean agreement between the reviewer's required concerns and `derive_concerns` 0.60; mean bundle 6.5 records / 7.1 kB; status CONFIDENT 114 · PARTIAL 46 · AMBIGUOUS 25 · ABSTAIN 27.

Class-level reading (per-metric and per-category aggregates only; individual cases were not opened, so the set stays usable): failures are dominated by concept recall (171 of 192 non-abstain cases below 0.5) and required-concern coverage (91), then mode (83). Adversarial UI-adjacent problems (14 cases: mode correct 4, recall 0.07) and media/dashboards/onboarding categories are weakest; tables, erp, accessibility and mobile are strongest on recall (0.27–0.37) but still under threshold. The reviewer's policy (bare React/Vue/Compose/SwiftUI → no platform) conflicts with the Phase 2 decision to infer web from React as INFERRED; no case failed on platform precision alone, so that policy difference does not explain the miss.

## J. Not done / honest limits

- Blind v2 thresholds missed on 5 of 8; the retrieval still surfaces the reviewer's expected concepts in a fifth of cases. The concept vocabulary (57 ids on 87 records) covers the base's own recurring principles, not the long tail reviewers name.
- Mode detection for problem statements reached 61 % on blind wording; the lexicon approach is precise but narrow.
- Abstain: 6 of 20 non-UI prompts activated (UI-adjacent tooling wording). Activation was frozen by design; this is recorded, not tuned.
- Real-model activation probe skipped (CLI unauthenticated) — again.
- Direction slots do not see the codebase's navigation model or theme polarity; the inspector does not extract them.
- Native TV/mobile builds were not possible on this machine; four cases rely on HTML twins.
- Torture-test agents ran while the skill was being edited; three re-verified after the edits, the others' verdicts describe the 00:28–00:40 build.
