# Kiosk and public touch displays (self-service, POS, check-in, ticketing, signage)

Standing, hurried, first-time users on a shared touch panel, often in bright or noisy environments, sometimes with legal accessibility requirements.

## Input and ergonomics

Touch only (occasionally a keypad, card reader, or headphone jack for audio navigation): targets ≥60 px with ≥16 px spacing; no hover, no drag precision, no pinch; controls placed within accessible reach ranges (interactive elements roughly 380–1220 mm above the floor for seated/standing users) and away from screen edges that bezels obscure; audio and visual feedback on every tap; glove-friendly where relevant.

## Structure

Attract/idle screen → hub of large labelled entry points → linear spokes with one primary action per screen; one task per screen; progress and a visible Cancel/Start over at every step; no scrolling to reach the primary action; idle timeout with a visible countdown that clears the session and personal data; confirmations for payments; receipts/next-step instructions as the final screen.

## Visual system

Body text ≥20 px, headings 32–48 px, high contrast for glare (target 7:1 for body), brand colour on the header and primary action, filled icons with labels (never icon-only), one corner language, illustration only where it explains a physical action (insert card, scan code), functional motion only; the attract loop is the single allowed ambient animation and it stops on touch.

## Content and language

Short instructions in plain language, large language switcher on the attract/hub screen, pictograms for physical actions, numbers in tabular figures (prices, times), local formats.

## Accessibility and compliance

Reach ranges and target sizes above; text scaling option or a large-text mode; audio guidance/headphone support and tactile keypad where regulations require (ADA/Section 508, EN 301 549); timeouts adjustable/extendable (WCAG 2.2.1); colour never alone; screen-reader mode when a keypad is present; privacy (no personal data left on screen; session cleared).

## Verification

Test on the real panel size and mounting height (or a scaled simulation), in bright light, with the idle timeout, with a glove/stylus if relevant, and through a full task from attract to receipt including cancel paths.
